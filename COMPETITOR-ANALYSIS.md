## Market Overview

The Delhi NCR non-surgical hair replacement market is growing rapidly, driven by rising male baldness rates, social media influence, and increasing acceptance of cosmetic hair solutions. The market includes both specialized studios (like Growig) and general hair salons offering basic patch services.

**Key Market Characteristics:**
- High geographic intent (searches include location: "Dwarka", "Gurgaon", "Delhi")
- Strong word-of-mouth and review influence on purchasing decisions
- Price-sensitive but quality-conscious consumer base
- Growing female market for wigs and toppers
- Seasonal peaks (pre-wedding season, summer for hair transplants being avoided)
